## MVP Scope (Strict)

Included (must build):  
\- Mandarat (mandatory onboarding)  
\- Projects  
\- Monthly Focus (simple selector only)  
\- Weekly Planning (task list, DnD priority)  
\- Today Execution (checkbox list)  
\- Weekly Review (summary \+ notes)

Excluded (do not build):  
\- Monthly Review  
\- Analytics dashboard  
\- Multiple yearly goals  
\- External calendar integrations  
\- AI recommendations  
\- Social/sharing features
